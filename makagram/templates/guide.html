Getting started

  • Register an account or sign in if you already have one
  • Go to Profile to set your nickname, avatar, and color
  • Your nickname is used everywhere — in chats, channels, and groups
  • You can also setup 2FA verification by your authenticator app, so if someone gets your password, they still can't access to your accout

Chats

  • Open Chats to see all users and start a private conversation
  • You can add users as friends — they appear at the top of your list
  • Block a user to prevent them from messaging you
  • Create a Group to chat with multiple people at once
  • Groups can be public (anyone can join) or private (invite only)
  • In private groups, the owner can invite members and kick them
  • You can mute notifications for any chat or group
  • Attach files and images to messages

Channels

  • Channels are one-way broadcast feeds — only admins can post
  • Subscribe to a channel to receive its posts in your Feed
  • Like posts from channels you're subscribed to
  • Create your own channel and invite admins to help you post
  • The channel owner can promote or demote admins
  • Mute a channel to stop receiving notifications from it

Lobbies

  • Lobbies are open public rooms — anyone can join and chat
  • Great for meeting new people or discussing topics openly
  • Messages are visible to all participants in real time

Feed

  • The Feed aggregates all activity relevant to you in one stream
  • Filter by Channels, Groups, Direct Messages, or Community posts
  • Like channel posts directly from the feed
  • Community tab shows system-wide announcements from admins

Notifications

  • The bell icon in the header shows your unread notifications
  • You get notified about new messages, channel posts, and group invites
  • Click a notification to go directly to the relevant chat or channel
  • Use "Mark all as read" to clear all at once
  • Mute specific chats or channels to stop receiving their notifications

Achievements

  • Earn achievements for being active on the platform
  • There are 3 rarity tiers of achievements:
    • Common (Gray) — Easy tasks that anyone can complete quickly
    Super (Yellow) — Challenging milestones that require time and effort
    Ultra (Pink) — Secret achievements created and awarded exclusively by admins (hidden from the achievements list until unlocked)
  • Every achievement features a unique name, description, icon, and custom suffix
  • Equip your unlocked achievements on the Achievements page to display a special suffix next to your nickname across the platform
